User-configurable Quick Actions would make the overview much more useful than a fixed selection.
A strong interaction model would be:
- Right-click any actionable button anywhere in the app.
- Choose Pin to Quick Actions.
- Pinned actions appear on the Overview page.
- Right-click a pinned action to Unpin it.
- Drag pinned actions to reorder them.
- Prevent duplicates automatically.
- Persist the selection and order between sessions.
- Optionally include Reset to Defaults.

Keep Quick Actions intentionally compact—perhaps 6–8 visible actions—then allow overflow or a “Show More” area. That prevents the Overview page from becoming another crowded utilities page.
There are a few important details worth planning:
- Toggles should reflect their live on/off state when pinned.
- Dropdown-based actions may need individual commands pinned rather than the whole dropdown.
- Destructive actions such as shutdown, emptying the Recycle Bin, or deleting files should retain their normal confirmation.
- Actions unavailable on the current system should appear disabled with a helpful tooltip.
- The right-click option should say Unpin from Quick Actions when already pinned.
This would let each user turn Overview into their own small control dashboard, which fits the app very well.
